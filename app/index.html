Help — how to use this page

  • Search: the bar at the top finds nutrients, foods, health topics and myths as you type, forgives typos, and understands local names like "nachni". Press / on a laptop to jump to it.
  • Health map: on the Health tab, pick one topic to see its foods, or up to three to see what helps all of them together (and any conflicts).
  • Compare: on the Foods tab, turn on Compare, tick 2 to 4 foods, then "See comparison" for a side-by-side table.
  • Save: tap ☆ on any card to add it to "My saved" on Home — nothing leaves your browser.
  • Share: tap 🔗 Share on any card for a link to that exact card, on WhatsApp or copied.
