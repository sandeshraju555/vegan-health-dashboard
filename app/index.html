About and disclaimer

About this site

This dashboard pulls together what reputable research says about plant-based nutrition into one searchable place - drawn from published studies, Indian food-composition data, and guidance from recognised health bodies, with every claim traced back to its source.

"Vegan" and "plant-based" are used interchangeably here - both mean food with no meat, fish, eggs, dairy, honey or other animal-derived ingredients. I have used the word "vegan" mostly in this dashboard.

Vegan food isn't automatically healthy. A packet of vegan biscuits or a plate of deep-fried snacks is still vegan - and still worth checking the label on. This dashboard tries to point toward the nutrient-dense choices within a vegan diet, not just the vegan-compliant ones.

It's written for two kinds of reader: someone vegan for the animals, and someone vegan (or vegan-curious) for their own health - the nutrition facts are the same either way.

Nothing here replaces professional advice, and nothing here should be your only reference point for a health decision. Use your own judgement too, and check with a vegan-friendly nutritionist or doctor if you're ever unsure.

This dashboard took shape with Claude (Anthropic's AI) lending a hand through both the data-consolidation and coding stages.

Why I built this

I turned vegan in 2002, after reading Maneka Gandhi's columns in The New Indian Express, and meeting a vegan in Bangalore who opened up this whole world for me.

The more I read about veganism in those early years, the more convinced I got - it's one of the better decisions I've made in life.

It wasn't easy at first, especially at social occasions. Veganism was a far more foreign idea back then than it is today. I remember a stretch where 7 out of 10 darshinis (south Indian fast-food restaurants) in Bangalore wouldn't even serve a plain black coffee without milk finding its way in somewhere - that was the kind of struggle it was, for example.

Being involved in animal rights and welfare eventually led me to start an equine welfare NGO in 2010, eight years after I turned vegan. The NGO runs on vegan principles too. My fellow trustee there, Mr. Arun Rangasamy, has been instrumental - both in my own life and in shaping the NGO into what it is today.

By profession, I've been a freelance French-English translator, a tourist guide, and am now a Registered Mutual Fund Distributor. I love reading and travelling, though I rarely get the time or occasion for either since many years now. What reading I do these days leans toward understanding equine and animal behaviour better - all in the hope of giving our rescued animals a life of dignity and freedom.

This dashboard is my attempt to make that kind of struggle a little easier for others - a single place to quickly check what a vegan diet already covers well, what needs a bit of planning, and where a supplement genuinely helps, so fewer people have to learn it the hard way, one awkward darshini order at a time.
